Why it matters

Your support opens the door.

The path to becoming a physician is hard for everyone — years of weed-out courses, MCAT prep that costs thousands before a single question is answered, and research, volunteer, and clinical hours stacked on top of a full course load and the exhaustion of proving, again and again, that you belong.

But for a student who is the first in their family to even attend college, that already-difficult path becomes something closer to a maze without a map. It isn't a matter of intelligence or work ethic. It's that no one in their life has walked this road before. No one has told them that clinical exposure needs to start early, that a personal statement is a strategic document and not a diary entry, that letters of recommendation are built through years of relationships rather than requested the week before a deadline. Students from underserved and first-generation backgrounds have to learn these things by accident, if they learn them at all — and by the time many do, it's already too late.

This is the exact gap MedRise Mentors was built to close.

We started as one medical student mentoring premed students who had no one else to ask the questions that mattered. Today, that idea has grown into a 501(c)(3) nonprofit reaching students across the United States, impacting more than a thousand premed students.

But mentorship alone isn't enough if it never reaches the students who need it most. That's why we build free academic resources — MCAT guides, application roadmaps, and clinical reference materials — so that no student is priced out of knowledge that others get for free through family and connections. And it's why we travel directly to underserved high schools and colleges to give presentations in person, to sit across from students and say, plainly:

This path is possible for you — and here is exactly how to walk it.

That travel, those resources, that mentorship — none of it happens without support. A donation to MedRise Mentors doesn't disappear into overhead and administrative bureaucracy. It becomes gas money to reach a rural school our mentors would otherwise never see. It becomes a printed guide placed in the hands of a student who needs more direction. Eventually, it becomes a scholarship that keeps a $300 exam fee from being the reason someone's dream in medicine ends before it ever begins.

Every physician alive today started as someone who believed, against real obstacles, that they could do this. For too many talented students, no one has ever told them that belief was justified. Your gift is what lets us be the ones who do — and what lets us show up, in person, for the students who need to hear it most. Thank you for helping us make more students RISE.

Where it goes

See the impact of your gift.

We're an all-volunteer nonprofit — so 100% of your gift goes directly to a student, never to salaries, overhead, or hidden admin fees. Every dollar helps make medicine more inclusive for first-generation and underserved students.

$50

Covers a set of official MCAT practice exams for a student who can't afford commercial prep.

$500

Covers a student's MCAT registration and a couple of full-length practice tests.

$1,000

Covers a large portion of a student's primary application — the first real gate into medical school.

$2,000

Covers a full round of a student's secondary applications, so cost never decides where they apply.
